Share your feedback

Share your feedback on the Public Interest Litigation W.P.(CRL) 2752/2026 before the Hon'ble High Court of Delhi, filed after the Satya Niketan PG hostel collapse. It takes about three minutes.

This is not a vote. The Court decides the case on the law and on the material the parties place before it, and it has not asked for public views. Whether any responses are placed before the Court is for the Court to decide.

Before you start

  • You will be asked for your name, address, PIN code and mobile number. Your email address and the last 4 digits of your Aadhaar are optional.
  • You do not need to sign in to Google to respond.
  • Never enter your full Aadhaar number, here or on any similar form.
  • Your feedback can be up to 1,500 characters (about 250 words). Please do not name or accuse any individual.
  • Please respond only once. If more than one response arrives with the same name, mobile number and email, only the first is counted. To correct your response, write to mail@kalpalaw.in rather than filling in the form again.
  • At the end of the form you will be asked to tick a declaration. The consent note below the form explains how your information is used.

Consent and use of your information

By submitting the form, you confirm that you are giving your information voluntarily and that it is true to the best of your knowledge. It is collected by Kalpa Law only to record public views on this petition, to remove duplicate responses and, if the Court permits, to place full responses before the Hon'ble High Court of Delhi. A full response includes everything you enter: your name, address, PIN code, mobile number, email and the last 4 digits of your Aadhaar (if given), and your feedback. Anything filed in Court is shared with the parties to the case. Your information is kept in the organiser's Google account and is not used for any other purpose. To correct or delete your response, or to withdraw your consent, write to mail@kalpalaw.in. If your response has already been filed in Court, it becomes part of the court record, and deleting it here will not remove it from there.
